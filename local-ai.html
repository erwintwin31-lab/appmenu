Before you start

This downloads a small language model and runs it on your device using WebGPU. Budget several hundred MB of downloads and around 1 GB or more of working memory; these are estimates, not measured limits. Exact transfer size is not yet verified. Older devices and browsers may not work.

Loading and generation can increase processor/GPU load, battery use and memory use. Your prompts are processed locally, not sent to an AI inference service. Model/runtime downloads contact third-party hosts, which receive normal network metadata such as your IP address. Downloaded files may remain in browser storage.

Answers may be wrong. This preview has no live web search and cannot install software or execute actions. Stop at any time. After five attempted questions the worker is stopped. Reloading resets this demonstration limit.

Runtime: jsDelivr/esm.run; model assets: WebLLM-configured Hugging Face/CDN hosts. No analytics are added by this page. To remove cached downloads, clear this site's browser storage. This can also remove other Appmenu demo preferences.
